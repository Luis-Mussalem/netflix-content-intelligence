# Netflix Content Intelligence 📊🎬

## 📌 Business Question
How has Netflix structured its content catalog over time, and what patterns can be identified in terms of content type, geography, and genres?

## 📂 Dataset
- Source: Kaggle – Netflix Movies and TV Shows
- Records: ~8,800 titles
- Period covered: 2015 - 2021

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Python** (Pandas, Matplotlib)
- **SQL** (SQLite-style queries)
- **Power BI**
- **Jupyter Notebook**
- **Git & GitHub**

## 🔍 Analysis Performed
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Data cleaning and transformation
- Time series analysis of catalog growth
- Content type comparison (Movies vs TV Shows)
- Geographic distribution of productions
- Genre frequency analysis

## 📊 Key Insights
- Movies represent approximately **70%** of Netflix’s catalog, while TV Shows account for **30%**.
- Most titles were added **after 2015**, with accelerated growth until **2018**, followed by a slowdown.
- The **United States** leads content production, followed by **India**, **United Kingdom**, **Canada**, and **France**.
- Netflix shows strong investment in **international content**, highlighting a global expansion strategy.
- Drama and international genres dominate the catalog.

## 💡 Business Interpretation
The data suggests an initial phase of aggressive catalog expansion focused on volume, followed by a more selective strategy emphasizing content optimization and international diversification.
